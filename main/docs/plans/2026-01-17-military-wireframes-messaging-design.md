# Military Wireframes Messaging Design

**Date**: 2026-01-17
**Status**: Approved
**Purpose**: Rebrand homepage from general 3D model processing to military equipment training platform

## Overview

Transform Mesh from a general geospatial 3D processing platform into a specialized military equipment intelligence training system. The platform will focus on training military personnel to identify and analyze military equipment through AI-powered wireframe analysis.

## Target Audience

**Primary**: Military trainees learning equipment identification and threat assessment
**Use Case**: Training & Education for combat readiness
**Tone**: Professional military — authoritative, mission-focused, intelligence-oriented

## Equipment Scope

All military equipment types including:
- Ground vehicles (tanks, APCs, IFVs)
- Aircraft (jets, helicopters, drones)
- Naval vessels (ships, submarines)
- Weapons systems (artillery, missiles)

## Core Messaging Strategy

### Positioning
- **From**: General 3D model processing for geospatial data
- **To**: AI-powered military equipment intelligence training platform
- **Key Angle**: Threat identification and tactical analysis for accelerated combat readiness

### Key Terminology Changes
- Remove: CAD software, 3D printing, commercial use, geospatial coordination
- Add: Threat identification, tactical intelligence, combat readiness, OPFOR training, vulnerability assessment

### AI Positioning
Frame AI features as "Intelligence Analysis" tools:
- AI-powered threat identification
- Automated component recognition
- Tactical assessment generation
- Intelligence-grade analysis
